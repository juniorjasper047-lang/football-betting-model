## Why the scope rule matters here

The system's two strategies depend on a repeatable model surface: club leagues with a stable pricing
market and a consistent set of teams. International windows break all three - squad turnover, no club
form baseline, and a much thinner odds market. Rather than pretend the model transfers, the card is
logged as a no-bet day.
