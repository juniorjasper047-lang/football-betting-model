## Honest note
`dc-blend-v1` is fitted to margin-stripped market prices, so **model probability ~= fair
probability**. There is **no independent edge** in this system. Expected value per leg is
approximately **-(bookmaker margin)**, roughly -5% on a 1X2 market, and it compounds across
parlay legs (a 3-leg slip is roughly a -15% EV bet). BSD's own `recommendations` block returns
`bet_favorite: false` for **all seven** fixtures today — the feed itself flags these prices as
too short.

This is a **hit-rate and discipline** strategy, not a value strategy. Expect to lose the parlay
about 4 times in 10. The Bet of the Day carries the larger stake precisely because it pays the
margin only once.
