- **CSKA Sofia vs Qarabag**: The only draw that hit — a classic 0-0 in a tight EL qualifier. European second legs with first-leg draws are draw-heavy and the model identified this correctly. Odds were estimated at the time but 3.70 proved solid value.

### What Didn't
- **Bulk draw-chasing in EL qualifiers**: 5 draw picks, only 1 hit. The model overestimated draw probability across the board. Home teams dominated (PAOK, Hradec Kralove, Maccabi TA, Midtjylland/Besiktas away).
- **Ferencvaros Home Win at 1.90**: Thin edge (0.27%) wasn't worth the risk. Ferencvaros went down to 10 men and conceded late — small edges at short odds are fragile.
- **Estimated odds**: All picks had "ODDS ESTIMATED" — model couldn't pull live odds at time of signal. This introduces edge inflation risk. Need to ensure live odds are available before firing.

### Model Adjustments
- **EL qualifier draw model needs recalibration**: Draw frequency in Q2 2nd legs is lower than model assumed, especially when a team carries a lead from leg 1 (Maccabi leading 5-0, Midtjylland down 0-1 but Besiktas strong).
- **Consider leg-1 context weighting**: Draws are more likely when tie is evenly balanced after leg 1, not when one side has a commanding lead.
- **Odds estimation flag**: Any pick with estimated odds should be treated as paper/research only until live odds confirmed.
