- **1X2 Home on class gaps landed 2/3.** Omonia 1-0 Lincoln and Besiktas 1-0 Hradec both hit.

### What Didn't
- **The low-total tail is where O2.5 died.** Dinamo Minsk 0-0 Braga, Shkendija 0-0 Hibernian, Motherwell 2-0 HJK, Besiktas 1-0 Hradec — cagey ties with one side sitting deep.
- **BSD data lag nearly cost two winners.** Tobol–Partizan showed `notstarted` in BSD at 22:00 UTC but had actually finished 1-2. ESPN/Sofascore fallback confirmed the result. Always cross-check 'notstarted' fixtures against a second source before leaving picks open.
- **KI Klaksvik–Lech Poznan genuinely postponed** (fog grounded Lech's flight; moved 24h to Aug 14). Both picks voided/pushed.
- **Team-name mismatch on MKK-Dnepr.** Tracker's 'MKK-Dnepr' = BSD's 'ML Vitebsk'. Needs a name-normalisation mapping.

### Model Adjustments
1. **Keep the must-chase multiplier for totals, but tighten the tail.** Sub-60% model-prob O2.5 tier bled.
2. **BTTS over O2.5 in European qualifiers.** BTTS outperformed O2.5 again.
3. **Add a team-name alias map** (MKK-Dnepr → ML Vitebsk, Dinamo Tirana → Dinamo City, Dynamo Kiev → Dynamo Kyiv).
4. **Track a true running bankroll.** Day book still opens at nominal 100.00; carry-forward is a known bug.
